About Us

H.FERR Consulting

Started in 2007, H.Ferr Consulting, LLC is a minority-owned interpretation agency based in Milwaukee Wisconsin. We curate experienced, honest and passionate interpreters with the highest level of professionalism and integrity. As an agency owned by language professionals, we provide an unmatched environment to guarantee quality control and vetting of the best interpreters and translators in Southeastern Wisconsin.

We strive to offer fully customizable language services to clients that go beyond words and build bridges across cultures. FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of the most common questions about our interpretation and translation services.

Why use a professional language interpretation and translation service? +

Working with a professional interpreter or translator brings significant benefits, especially in industries sensitive to liability issues.

Medical

  • Assures effective communication between patients and providers.
  • Prevents negative results caused by working with unqualified interpreters.
  • Reduces liability due to incompetent interpreting.
  • Helps providers gain the trust and respect of their LEP patients.

Legal

  • Ensures rapport between legal providers and their clients.
  • Minimizes confusion and misunderstandings.

Business

If your company has a large number of limited English-speaking employees, H.Ferr Consulting can help ensure all employees understand company policies, procedures, and benefits.

What qualifications do your interpreters have? +

H.Ferr interpreters must either be native speakers of the foreign language or have native fluency in the foreign language. They must also display native fluency in English.

In addition to fluency in at least two languages, our interpreters are specially trained to interpret both medical and legal terminology. They also receive training on HIPAA regulations and all sign a Code of Ethics.

If your industry requires unique interpretation skills, let us know. We will work with you to make sure our interpreters have additional preparation specific to your needs.

How far ahead do I need to schedule an appointment? +

While we appreciate as much advance notice as you can give us, we do our best to get an interpreter to you promptly no matter when you need one.

We recognize that many appointments are scheduled at the last minute. Because of our large staff and experienced scheduling team, we welcome the opportunity to send an interpreter even for short-notice appointments.

We can generally have an interpreter to your location in as little as 30 minutes. We are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

To what locations does H.Ferr Consulting provide services? +

H.Ferr Consulting services clients throughout Wisconsin. We provide services everywhere from home visits and attorney's offices to hospitals and clinics around the state.

What information do I need to have when I call to schedule an appointment? +

We will need the date, time, and location of your appointment. We will also ask for the client or patient's name and the name of the responsible party.

If you have any special requests, please let us know. We are always willing to accommodate special requests.

What are H.Ferr Consulting's rates? +

Rates vary based on the needs of individual clients. Please call H.Ferr Consulting at 414.261.4337 or email us at info@hferr.com so that we can provide a custom price quote for you.

What if I need to cancel an appointment? +

Please notify us as soon as possible if an appointment is canceled. If the cancellation is received less than 24 hours before the scheduled appointment time, the minimum two-hour charge will be invoiced.

Does H.Ferr Consulting provide phone interpreting services? +

Yes, we are happy to provide interpreting services by phone. For example, clients frequently use our services to schedule or confirm appointments or to provide instructions prior to an appointment.

Please call us to get more information about this service.

How do I schedule? +

That's up to you. You can call or email your request. If you need an immediate response, please call us at 414.261.4337 .
