About WisconSign

Your trusted partner for every signing

At WisconSign, we make the loan signing process simple, secure, and stress-free. Founded in 2024, we are a bilingual (English–Spanish) loan signing and notary service proudly serving the state of Wisconsin.

Our Services

Notary services: advice and procedures

01

Jurats

Verification of identity and voluntary signing. The signer swears or affirms that the information in the document is true.

02

Oaths & Affirmations

Administration of formal promises — sworn (oaths) or non-religious (affirmations) — for legal or official purposes.

03

Wills & Trusts

Professional notarization of estate planning documents to provide clarity, protection, and legal certainty for your assets.

04

Medical Documents

Certification of medical directives, consent forms, and health-related paperwork to ensure they meet legal standards.

05

DMV Forms

Notarization of vehicle-related documents required by the Department of Motor Vehicles for transfers, releases, and ownership verification.

06

General Notarizations

Fast and reliable notarization for a wide variety of legal, personal, and business documents.
